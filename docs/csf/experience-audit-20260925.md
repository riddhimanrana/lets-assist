# CSF experience audit, September 25, 2026

This audit covers member submissions, officer review, Sheets, semester transitions, class lifecycle, graduation, and partner clubs. Local changes live on `codex/csf-experience-audit` in both repositories. The root started at `e4fd3c2`; private Development started at `ac6c470`, whose tree matches the pinned `a294ee5` source.

This is an implementation candidate. It has not been merged, deployed, or installed by the chapter. No live student records or Google destinations were changed.

## Operational boundaries reviewed

- Pending point claims do not increase verified totals. Approval, correction, revision checks, proof authorization, and credit uniqueness remain server-enforced.
- Reviewer splitting retains its existing queue and range semantics. It does not silently broaden a filtered application queue or assign unauthorized reviewers.
- Sheets remain import/export channels. Immutable source previews, identity reconciliation, authorization checks, leases, and reviewed commit rules remain in place. These changes enable no destination or automatic decision release.
- Closing snapshots membership outcomes. Starting another term does not close the previous one. Reopening preserves closure history and permits a new audited closure revision.
- Senior recognition remains derived from completed semesters. Officers can review it under Settings, Download reports, Senior recognition. Archiving a class does not award recognition or replace the separate graduated-data retention workflow.
- Clubs retain canonical identity and semester-specific standing. Historical standing is not overwritten by selecting another semester. Club spreadsheet links remain references.
- Private proof is rendered from authorized URLs. No external image service receives evidence, and viewer controls do not rewrite uploads.
